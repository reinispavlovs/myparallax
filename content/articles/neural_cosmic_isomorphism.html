Tribunal reading of the isomorphism question

The research question is whether neural networks and the cosmic web stand in a relation of structural isomorphism. On the supplied corpus, that relation is unresolved as a scientific claim and unsubstantiated as an isomorphism in the strict sense required by the skeptic critique (a specific quantitative comparison with a stated metric and citation). The study reports, via LLM categorization of 20 abstracts, a 100.0 inconclusive share and a 0.0 share in supports_veridical, null_result, and neuro_mechanism.

This pattern is consistent with a corpus that does not, under the supplied classifier, yield a veridical-support bin, a dedicated null-result bin, or a neuro-mechanism bin for the isomorphism question. It is not treated here as a settled dismissal of the question, because an inconclusive label is not the same as a null result, and because the model_note describes LLM categorization of abstracts rather than a hand-verified systematic review.

Where individual titles discuss brain graphs, connectomes, distance-dependent connectivity, or multiscale reorganization, those titles may be relevant to network description of nervous systems. Relevance to brain networks is not the same as a measured match to cosmic-web structure. No supplied number is a similarity score, a shared-degree exponent, a mutual information value, or any other isomorphism metric. This draft therefore does not attach such a metric.

Project Parallax does not collapse a non-mainstream analogy into falsehood merely because consensus is absent. It also does not upgrade resemblance talk into supported structure. The honest status on this file is: corpus analysis complete; classifier output uniformly inconclusive; isomorphism claim not established.
