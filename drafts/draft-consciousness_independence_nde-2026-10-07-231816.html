Unsubstantiated

A general model in which consciousness is shown to operate independently of the brain, or in which non-local mind is established as the best explanation of perceptual anomalies, is unsubstantiated by this dataset. The file does not contain a verified, controlled demonstration of information acquired without sensory or inferential channels. Testimony in abstracts is not treated as proof.

How the categories should and should not be read

The skeptic critique supplied with the data is adopted as a methodological bound. Category boundaries are fuzzy. A pattern of reported near-death perceptions, if present in titles, does not by itself distinguish non-local consciousness from hypoxia, temporal-lobe activation, REM intrusion, or related substrates. Percentages are a rough corpus-level signal, not a peer-reviewed effect size. Independent recurring reports remain an observational dataset. Absence of consensus is not evidence of absence. Testimony is not proof, and testimony is not zero evidence. Competing explanations remain unresolved. The tribunal role is evaluation rather than dismissal.

Accordingly, this draft does not collapse the supports_veridical case or the six observational_signal items into inconclusive solely because a mainstream consensus is absent. It also does not promote those items to proven status. The single supports_veridical classification and the observational cluster are consistent with the claim that anomalous and end-of-life reports continue to appear in the literature. The neuro_mechanism cluster is consistent with the claim that brain-based accounts are also still in play. The large inconclusive share, the LLM-only method, and the presence of thematically distant papers leave the independence question unresolved.

Draft finding for Project Parallax

On the supplied illustrative meta-analysis, independence of consciousness remains unresolved. One abstract in 88 is classified as supports_veridical; six are classified as observational_signal; seven as neuro_mechanism; seventy-four as inconclusive; none as null_result. That distribution is consistent with a literature in which anomalous perceptual reports and end-of-life lucidity continue to be written up, while brain-based models (REM intrusion, migraine aura, neurochemical resemblance, dying-moment dream) are also written up, and while most retrieved abstracts do not decide the question. Non-local mind is not established by these figures. Dismissal of the observational cluster solely for lack of consensus is not warranted by the tribunal rules used here. Human fact-check should re-read the primary papers, test relevance of off-topic rows, and replace LLM labels before any publication use.
