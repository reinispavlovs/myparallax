What this corpus can and cannot bear

Independence of consciousness, including non-local mind models, is a hypothesis about whether some awareness, perception, or identity can occur without, or not wholly as, presently measured brain activity. Reported perceptual anomalies in this neighbourhood include near-death experience (NDE) reports, claims of veridical perception during coma or cardiac arrest, terminal lucidity, and related end-of-life phenomenology. This draft does not treat any of those as established facts.

Proven

Nothing in the supplied illustrative meta-analysis is treated as proven. A 1.0% classification share, a single labelled case report, and an 88.2% inconclusive share do not establish that mind is independent of the brain, nor do they establish that all such reports are illusory.

Supported (narrow, classification-level only)

The dataset classifies 1 of 102 sources (1.0%) as supports_veridical: Sepioni, Metastasio, and Monti, “A distressing near-death experience with veridical perceptions during coma: psychiatric and clinical reflections from a rare case” (year listed as 2026). That label is an LLM category applied to an abstract, not an independent audit of the clinical file. At tribunal level this is, at most, supported as a classified case-level report of alleged veridical perception during coma. It is not treated as replication, not as a population estimate, and not as proof of non-local mind.

Suggestive

Several features of the corpus are suggestive as observational signals, not as mechanism:

  • The dataset records a non-zero class of sources labelled as supporting veridical content, rather than forcing every anomalous report into a null bin.
  • Recurring NDE, end-of-life, and related phenomenological titles appear alongside instrument-building work (including a veridical NDE scale paper classified as inconclusive).
  • Independent recurring reports, if later confirmed as independent, would themselves be data. This draft does not verify independence, duplication, or clinical quality of those reports.

Suggestive is not supported in the strong sense, and it is not proven.

Unresolved

The dominant corpus result is unresolved. The dataset classifies 90 of 102 sources (88.2%) as inconclusive and 11 of 102 (10.8%) as neuro_mechanism. Zero sources (0.0%) are classified as null_result. A zero null-result class is not evidence of a positive effect; it is consistent with search strategy, label design, abstract-only coding, or classifier bias. Competing accounts remain open, including hypoxia, temporal-lobe activation, REM-sleep intrusion, anaesthetic and EEG-state models, neurochemical similarity to psychedelic reports, and a dying-moment dream hypothesis. The supplied skeptic critique states that a pattern of reported near-death perceptions does not by itself distinguish non-local consciousness from shared neurological substrates.

Titles in the inventory also show substantial topic scatter (for example veterinary stunning, unrelated medical case reports, oncology, genetics, and public-health papers). That scatter is consistent with noisy retrieval plus LLM labelling. It further leaves the mapping from “NDE-relevant evidence” to “102-item corpus” unresolved.

Unsubstantiated

The following are unsubstantiated on this dataset alone: that consciousness has been shown to operate independently of the brain; that non-local mind is the best explanation of NDEs; that veridical perception during coma is a general phenomenon; that a 1.0% supports_veridical share is an effect size; and that 0.0% null_result means disconfirming studies do not exist.

Category-by-category reading

Supports_veridical (n = 1; 1.0%)

The sole source so labelled is a rare-case clinical/psychiatric reflection on a distressing NDE with reported veridical perceptions during coma. Case reports can place a claim on the evidentiary table. They do not, by themselves, isolate non-locality from residual sensory leakage, reconstruction after recovery, interviewer expectation, or undocumented clinical detail. The tribunal records the classification and keeps the ontological inference unresolved.

Null_result (n = 0; 0.0%)

No source in this illustrative classification was labelled null_result. That empty bin is a property of the labelled corpus, not a finding that controlled tests have failed to falsify non-local models, and not a finding that they have succeeded.

Neuro_mechanism (n = 11; 10.8%)

Sources so labelled in the inventory include EEG and anaesthesia-related work, brain “twilight zone” discussion, G-force EEG alteration, cardiac-arrest EEG patterns, REM-intrusion and migraine-aura associations with NDE reports, arousal-system hypotheses, a dying-moment dream hypothesis, and a large-scale neurochemical-similarity study of NDE narratives. These items are consistent with brain-based accounts of some anomalous reports. They do not, on the supplied metadata, close off the possibility that some reports remain unexplained by those accounts. Mechanism papers and anomalous-report papers can coexist without one deleting the other.

Inconclusive (n = 90; 88.2%)

This bin mixes NDE measurement papers, coma and resuscitation outcome studies, terminal-lucidity and end-of-life reviews, psychedelic–NDE comparisons, and many abstracts whose titles suggest weak or no topical fit. Under tribunal rules, “inconclusive” means the coded abstract did not clearly support veridical non-local perception, a null result, or a specified neural mechanism. It does not mean the underlying testimony is worthless, and it does not mean the underlying testimony is true.

Tribunal disposition

On the supplied illustrative meta-analysis of 102 classified abstracts:

  • Proven: none.
  • Supported: only the dataset’s single supports_veridical classification of one coma-related case report of alleged veridical perception; supported as a labelled report, not as a demonstrated non-local mechanism.
  • Suggestive: a mixed observational neighbourhood of NDE, terminal-lucidity, end-of-life, and related titles, plus the fact of a non-zero veridical-support class. Recurring testimony is data; it is not self-authenticating.
  • Unresolved: the 88.2% inconclusive majority; the 10.8% neuro_mechanism class; the 0.0% null_result class; competition between non-local mind models and brain-substrate models (including hypoxia, temporal-lobe activation, REM intrusion, EEG-state, and neurochemical accounts); and the topical heterogeneity of the retrieval set.
  • Unsubstantiated: any claim that this corpus shows consciousness to be independent of the brain, or that it shows all anomalous perception to be fully explained by known neural mechanisms.

Human fact-check should re-read full texts, re-label off-topic hits, resolve duplicate records, and separate testimony, instrument papers, mechanism papers, and unrelated retrieval noise before any later Project Parallax version is treated as citable.
