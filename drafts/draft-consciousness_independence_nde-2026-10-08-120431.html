Non-local mind models in light of the corpus

Non-local or independence-of-consciousness models would gain weight if veridical perception during documented unresponsiveness were repeatedly corroborated under conditions that tightly constrain ordinary sensory and memory explanations. This corpus lists 1 item in supports_veridical and 0 in null_result. That imbalance is a corpus-level signal only. It is consistent with the continued presence of veridical-perception claims in the literature stream that passed the relevance gate; it is not a substitute for controlled adjudication.

Neurological models would gain weight if NDE-like reports tracked REM intrusion, migraine aura, arousal physiology, or neurochemical states in a way that accounted for content, timing, and any alleged veridical elements. This corpus lists 7 neuro_mechanism items, a larger labelled share than supports_veridical. That is consistent with an active brain-based literature; it does not show that every observational report has been absorbed by those models.

Observational_signal items (6) sit between those poles. Terminal lucidity and end-of-life experience reports are relevant to mind–brain timing: they raise the question of organised mentation when the brain is expected to be failing. Comparative DMT work raises the question of shared phenomenology without settling ontology. Project Parallax records these as data for analysis, not as a licence to declare a non-local conclusion and not as a licence to discard the reports.
