That mix is consistent with a noisy retrieval set and with the high inconclusive rate. It is not, by itself, evidence that consciousness is independent of the brain, nor evidence that all anomalous reports reduce to one neural mechanism.

Limits on interpretation for non-local mind models

Non-local mind models are sometimes invoked when people report perceptions that appear to occur during severely reduced clinical responsiveness. The supplied classifier output does not distinguish those reports from hypoxia, temporal-lobe activation, REM intrusion, or other candidate substrates, a point already stated in the dataset’s skeptic critique. Zero records were labeled null_result, which the dataset does not explain; that empty bin should not be read as a failed replication program or as positive support for independence of consciousness.

Category boundaries are described in the dataset as inherently fuzzy. LLM labeling of abstracts is not equivalent to blinded outcome extraction, preregistered inclusion criteria, or independent verification of alleged veridical details.
