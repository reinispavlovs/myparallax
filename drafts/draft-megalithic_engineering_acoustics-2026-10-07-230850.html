Physics framing without added constants

Air and dense rock differ strongly in characteristic impedance. The supplied model therefore labels the interface a high-reflection barrier. High airborne reflection is consistent with little transmitted acoustic intensity into the solid under a simple normal-incidence mismatch. That is why a large reflection percentage does not, on its own, suggest efficient driving of stone resonance from air sound. Efficient driving would require a coupling mechanism that this dataset does not quantify.

The arXiv excerpt, by contrast, discusses enhanced transmission associated with engineered boundary impedance at a slit, which is a different geometry from a blank reflecting face. Mixing a near-total reflection result with an extraordinary-transmission slit result without a shared model is unresolved and is not done numerically here.
