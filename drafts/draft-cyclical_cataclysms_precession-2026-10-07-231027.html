Tribunal method

Project Parallax treats this file as an evidence tribunal, not as an academic gatekeeper. Evidence categories are scored independently. Absence of academic consensus is not treated as evidence of falsehood. Presence of testimony is not treated as evidence of truth. Large numbers of independent reports, when they exist, are themselves data and would be analysed statistically; this dataset does not contain such a report corpus. Non-mainstream material is not collapsed to “inconclusive” solely because a mainstream consensus is absent. Language is limited to proven, supported, suggestive, unresolved, and unsubstantiated. Definitive wording such as “proves,” “confirms,” or “demonstrates conclusively” is not used.
