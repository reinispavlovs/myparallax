Working position for later review

The illustrative model is consistent with a high-reflection air–stone boundary under the ordinary mismatch equation. A located study reports that boundary-impedance engineering can, in a single subwavelength slit, be associated with strongly enhanced transmission. Linking those two statements to megalithic granite as an intentional resonant system is, on the present record, unresolved. Treating the reflection percentage as proof of ancient intent would overstep the data. Discarding the extraordinary-transmission literature solely for lack of archaeological consensus would also overstep the tribunal rule set. Human fact-check is required before any public use.
