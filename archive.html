How to read this

“Current” is what the Tribunal shows today. “Superseded” versions were replaced by a newer one on the date shown.

“Human-reviewed” means a person checked the article against its sources before publication. “AI draft, not reviewed” is an earlier machine-written draft, kept only for transparency. Do not cite it as a finding.
