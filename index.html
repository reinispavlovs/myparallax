The author's hypothesis, not a result

The idea that drives this project

Many people who report a near-death experience describe it as more real than ordinary life. That is a subjective report, and the site treats it as one. It is also the reason the question is worth testing: if consciousness is more than a biological body, the evidence should be visible somewhere.

The hypothesis is that consciousness may be non-local, and that ancient builders may have known something about resonance, cycles and the sky that was later fragmented and misread. Several of these claims are contested, and the pages below say which ones.

The method is parallax: look at the same questions (consciousness, history, the cosmos) from several viewpoints, and mark honestly where each one stands. The aim is not to assume the answer. It is to find out, and to keep anything that bears on the question from being lost.
